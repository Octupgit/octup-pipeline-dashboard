01  Vanguard

Minimal typographic

Vanguard

Hours stacked over minutes at the largest size the device has a font for — roughly three times the digit height of a stock digital face. The minutes take the blue accent so the time reads as two numbers rather than one four-digit block.

Pick this one if you mostly want to know the time and resent the clutter. Everything else is a single row along the bottom, with one hairline under it for the step goal.

  • Steps + goal hairline
  • Temperature + conditions
  • Watch battery
  • Status dots

02  Flux

Data dashboard

Flux

Six metrics in a grid, weather on the top right, a full-width step-goal bar and today's daylight window along the bottom. The time is left-aligned and deliberately smaller here, because on this face it is one field among several rather than the subject.

Pick this one if you open glance widgets several times a day. It is the only face that carries all three groups you asked for at once, and the only one with sunrise and sunset.

  • Steps
  • Heart rate
  • Body Battery
  • Calories
  • Floors
  • Intensity min week
  • Weather temp, low/high
  • Move bar

03  Orbit

Goal rings

Orbit

Two concentric gauges: steps against today's goal outside, Body Battery inside. Both are questions of "how full am I", which a ring answers at a glance and a number does not. The matching numbers sit along the bottom in the same colours, so the rings never become decoration.

Pick this one if you chase a daily step goal or watch your Body Battery drain through the day. Weakest face for planning — it has temperature but no forecast and no sun times.

  • Steps ring vs goal
  • Body Battery ring
  • Heart rate
  • Temperature

orbit/source/OrbitView.mc

04  Meridian

Analog hybrid

Meridian

A real analog dial with sixty ticks, a creeping hour hand and a Body Battery arc wrapped around the outside. Four values sit in a strip underneath with no captions, identified only by colour — this face is meant to look like a watch rather than a readout.

Pick this one if the Venu X1 is also a dress watch. The second hand runs only while the screen is awake; there is no point animating a hand nobody is looking at.

  • Body Battery arc
  • Steps
  • Heart rate
  • Temperature
  • Watch battery

How close this is to the real thing

Positions, sizes, colours and goal arithmetic are taken straight from the Monkey C, so the composition is accurate. The type is not: Garmin's built-in fonts ship on the device and cannot be loaded here, so each one is matched by eye to a Barlow weight at a similar height. Expect glyph widths to shift by a few pixels in the simulator — the bottom rows are the places to check.

Two things no watch face can show, because Connect IQ exposes no API for them: your calendar, and last night's sleep score. Notification count is available and is one of the status dots; the rest of your day planning has to come from a glance widget.
